White Balance / Color Temperature Reference

An interactive Kelvin scale with real-world light sources — from candlelight to open shade. Drag the slider, see the color, and match your camera's white balance to the scene instead of fighting it in post.

Common sources

Source≈ KelvinNotes
Candlelight1,900 KVery warm; white balance rarely used
Tungsten / incandescent3,200 KClassic indoor practicals; camera "tungsten" preset
Fluorescent / office4,000 KCan carry a green spike — check tint, not just temp
Direct daylight, noon5,600 KCamera "daylight" preset
Overcast sky6,500 KSoft, neutral-cool
Open shade / blue sky7,500 KNoticeably blue without correction

Swatch colors are approximations of black-body radiation for reference — real sources (especially fluorescents and LEDs) shift green/magenta too, which Kelvin alone doesn't describe. When mixing sources, gel or replace the odd one out rather than splitting the difference.
