Shutter Angle ↔ Shutter Speed

Cinema cameras think in shutter angle, hybrid cameras in shutter speed. Convert between them at your exact frame rate — and see the classic angles (90°, 144°, 180°, 360°) at a glance.

Common angles at 24 fps

180° → 1/48 s — the cinematic default.  90° → 1/96 s — crisper motion (Saving Private Ryan look).  360° → 1/24 s — maximum blur, useful in low light.  144° → 1/60 s — kills fluorescent flicker under 60 Hz lighting.

Math: exposure time (sec) = (angle ÷ 360) × (1 ÷ fps). A 180° shutter exposes for half of each frame's duration — at 24 fps that's 1/48 s, the look audiences read as "cinema". Use 144° (1/60) under 60 Hz lights or 172.8° (1/50) under 50 Hz lights to avoid flicker.
