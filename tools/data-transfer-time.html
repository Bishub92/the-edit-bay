File Transfer Time Calculator

Offloading a full 1 TB card before the next shoot day? Enter the data size and pick your connection — this estimates the transfer time using real-world sustained speeds, not the numbers on the box.

Math: time (sec) = data size (bytes, decimal) ÷ (sustained MB/s × efficiency). Preset speeds are realistic sustained rates, not the theoretical maximums printed on packaging (USB 3.0’s 5 Gbps headline is 625 MB/s in theory; real transfers land near 350 MB/s). Efficiency accounts for protocol overhead, thermal throttling, and many small files — thousands of clips copy far slower than one big file at the same byte rate.
