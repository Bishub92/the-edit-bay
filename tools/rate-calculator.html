Freelance Rate Calculator

Turn your income goal into a day rate that actually covers expenses, taxes, and time off. Most freelancers undercharge because they divide salary by 365 — this does the real math for videographers, editors, and shooters.

Billable days = days you can actually invoice. Start from 260 weekdays, then subtract vacation, sick days, holidays, and non-billable admin/marketing days — 180–220 is realistic for most freelancers. The margin covers taxes, slow months, and gear replacement.
