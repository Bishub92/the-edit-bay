Upload Time Calculator

How long will that export take to reach YouTube, Frame.io, or a client's Dropbox? Enter file size and your upload speed to find out — with real-world overhead included.

Math: time (sec) = file size (bits) ÷ (speed × efficiency). Bits per byte use 8 × decimal GB (the way ISPs quote speed), and the efficiency factor accounts for protocol overhead and congestion — ISPs advertise downstream best-case, uploads on cable/fiber usually land lower. Common checks: 10 Mbps up is typical US cable; symmetrical fiber can hit 100+ Mbps.
