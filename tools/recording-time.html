Recording Time Estimator

How much footage fits on your card or drive at a given bitrate — or how much storage a shoot day will need. Essential for wedding shooters, event videographers, and anyone who's ever run out of card mid-ceremony.

Uses decimal gigabytes (1 GB = 1,000,000,000 bytes), matching how card and drive manufacturers label capacity. Real-world record time runs ~3–5% under the math due to file-system overhead — round down for safety on critical shoots. ProRes figures are Apple's published specs for 1080p29.97.
