Export Settings Cheat Sheet

Copy-paste export presets for every major platform — resolution, codec, bitrate, and audio, verified against current platform recommendations. Every platform re-encodes your upload, so the goal is giving it a clean, high-quality source.

YouTube (long-form)

SettingValue
ContainerMP4 (moov atom at front / "Fast Start")
Video codecH.264, High Profile, progressive
AudioAAC-LC, 48 kHz, 320 kbps stereo
Frame rateMatch source (24 / 25 / 30 / 60)
1080p bitrate8 Mbps (30 fps) · 12 Mbps (60 fps)
1440p bitrate16 Mbps (30 fps) · 24 Mbps (60 fps)
4K bitrate35–45 Mbps (30 fps) · 53–68 Mbps (60 fps)
EncodingVBR, 2-pass; chroma 4:2:0

Practical tip: uploading ~50% above YouTube's published floor (e.g. 12 Mbps for 1080p30) gives the re-encode chain more headroom and visibly reduces compression artifacts on detailed footage.

YouTube Shorts · TikTok · Instagram Reels

SettingValue
Resolution1080 × 1920 (9:16)
Container / codecMP4 / H.264 High Profile
Frame rate30 fps (match source; 60 only for action)
Video bitrate8–12 Mbps VBR
AudioAAC, 48 kHz (44.1 accepted), 192–320 kbps
ColorSDR / Rec.709 (avoid HDR unless you know the workflow)
Loudness≈ −14 LUFS integrated, −1 dBTP true peak

Keep key text and faces inside the central safe zone — interface overlays crop the top ~250 px and bottom ~250 px in feed views, and profile grids crop to square.
