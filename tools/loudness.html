Audio Loudness (LUFS) Guide

The correct integrated loudness and true-peak targets for every major platform — plus a checker that tells you whether your mix will get turned down. Because every platform normalizes, and a crushed master just gets quieter.

Will my mix be turned down?

LUFS and LKFS are the same unit (European vs. US broadcast naming). These targets are playback levels, not creative mandates — a well-balanced master at −9 LUFS keeps its character after normalization; a mix strangled to exactly −14 gains nothing. Always verify with a true-peak meter: inter-sample peaks can exceed 0 dBTP even when samples don't.
