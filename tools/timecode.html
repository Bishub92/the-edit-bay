Frame / Timecode Converter

Convert frame counts to HH:MM:SS:FF timecode and back, at any frame rate — including 23.976 and 29.97. For onset notes, edit lists, and VFX frame references.

Uses non-drop-frame counting. NTSC rates (23.976, 29.97, 59.94) are handled with their exact fractional values (24000/1001 etc.), so frame counts stay accurate over long durations. True drop-frame timecode (used in US broadcast) skips frame numbers periodically — this tool does not apply drop-frame numbering.
