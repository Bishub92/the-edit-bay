Depth of Field Calculator

How much of your shot will be in acceptable focus? Enter your lens, aperture, sensor size, and how far away your subject is — get the hyperfocal distance, near and far focus limits, and total depth of field.

Math: hyperfocal distance H = f² ÷ (N × c) + f. Near limit = s(H − f) ÷ (H + (s − f)); far limit = s(H − f) ÷ (H − (s − f)). When the subject is at or beyond the hyperfocal distance, the far limit extends to infinity. This is the standard simplified thin-lens model — it describes acceptable sharpness at typical viewing, not pixel-perfect focus, and it breaks down at macro distances and with very fast lenses where focus breathing and field curvature matter.
