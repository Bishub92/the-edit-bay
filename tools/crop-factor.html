Crop Factor & Equivalent Focal Length

A 35mm lens doesn't look like a 35mm on every camera. Pick your sensor size, enter the lens focal length, and get the full-frame equivalent plus the horizontal field of view.

Math: equivalent focal length = focal length × crop factor (36 ÷ sensor width). Horizontal FOV = 2 × arctan(sensor width ÷ 2 ÷ focal length). Note: crop factor only changes framing — the lens's depth of field and aperture behavior don't magically change, though the longer working distance does affect how backgrounds look.
