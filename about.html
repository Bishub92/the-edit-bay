About The Edit Bay

The Edit Bay is a collection of free, no-nonsense calculators and reference tools for people who make video — editors, shooters, producers, YouTubers, and freelancers.

What we believe

Production math shouldn't require a forum deep-dive. Bitrates, loudness targets, export specs, and budgets are settled engineering — they deserve tools that just give the right answer.

Everything here runs entirely in your browser. We don't have accounts, we don't track you, and we don't sell your data — because we never collect it.

How we stay free

Down the road, the site may recommend gear through affiliate links (clearly labeled) — that's how tools like this stay free without charging you or selling ads against your attention. Recommendations will only ever be gear we'd use ourselves.
