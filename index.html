Why these tools exist

Every one of these calculators answers a question working editors, shooters, and producers ask constantly: what bitrate do I need? how much card space is that? what should I charge? will YouTube crush my audio? We built them to give correct answers fast — the math is checked against published platform specs and broadcast standards.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or tracked.

Are the numbers trustworthy?

Bitrate math follows the standard file-size formula used across the industry; platform presets reflect current published recommendations (YouTube's upload encoding guide, EBU R128, ATSC A/85); loudness targets match each platform's documented normalization levels.
